About Us

Online platforms have emerged as a promising solution for providing access to legal services for citizens in India. So here’s another onboard platform which will provide people with legal service options at their fingertip. The eMarket Place platform is a novel solution to bridge the gap between the demand and supply of legal services in India. The law and order system seems to be in an unorganized manner. To overcome the law related drawbacks, the project focuses to develop an onboard platform like arbitrators, notaries, document writers, advocates, etc. The development will focus on a platform wherein the description of various legal service providers will be provided to the citizens of India who are not aware of the proper documents, laws and other legal services and hence may end up having more trouble in their lives.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to creating an innovative eMarketplace that seamlessly connects citizens with a diverse range of legal practitioners, including Advocates, Arbitrators, Mediators, Notaries, and Document Writers.

Our Plan

Continuously enhance the user interface for an intuitive and seamless experience.Forge partnerships with legal institutions, organizations, and regulatory bodies to ensure compliance and credibility.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a digital space where legal services are accessible to all. We envision a website that serves as a beacon of legal support, providing transparent, and efficient solutions for individuals and businesses alike
